Availability and pricing

Morph 1.0 powers Sign Morph in Talksign Canvas: upload a signing video, choose a person, download. It runs on the same model as Echo 2.0.

It takes a signing video of any length, frame rate or size, plus one photo of the new person; a neutral, front-facing photo works best. Morph redraws every frame, following the hands, body and face, and returns a photorealistic video at up to 720p with the same frames, timing and size, plus a hand report for review. It has been tested on American Sign Language (ASL) signing.

Processing takes about a minute of GPU time per second of video, so Morph is for produced content, not live calls.

In Talksign Canvas, Sign Morph uses credits from your plan or top-ups: 5 credits per second of video at 720p, the default, or 3 credits per second at 480p, with a 10-second minimum. You’re only charged when a render succeeds.

Through the developer API, Morph costs $0.12 per second at 720p and $0.06 per second at 480p, paid from a prepaid wallet with a $50 minimum top-up and volume discounts. To get started, request API access or email dev@talksign.co.

Use cases

Sign language video has always been tied to the person who recorded it. Morph keeps the signing and changes the person, so teams can publish produced sign language content in new ways:

01

Media and entertainment

Re-present signed episodes, trailers and social clips with one presenter, without booking the studio again.

02

Brands and marketing

Give your brand one signing presenter across campaigns and product videos, even when different people signed them on different days.

03

Education

Publish a whole course with one familiar presenter, so learners follow the same face from lesson to lesson.

04

Public services

Share signed announcements and guidance with an official presenter, while the staff who signed them keep their faces private.

05

Healthcare

Produce signed patient information with a consistent presenter, and let the signers who recorded it stay anonymous.

06

Creators and community

Deaf creators sign as a character or avatar, with their own signing, timing and expression intact. The signing still belongs to them.

Trust and safety

Morph may only be used with the consent of both the signer in the video and the person in the photo. Outputs are machine-generated and should be labelled as such, and impersonation and deceptive use are not allowed. Re-rendering changes appearance, not authorship: the signing stays the original signer’s work. We’re preparing a blind study where fluent Deaf ASL users judge whether meaning, clarity and naturalness are kept, and we’ll publish the results.

Frequently asked questions

When should I use Morph?

Use Morph for produced sign language content: a consistent presenter, a creator’s character, or a signer who wants to keep their face private. It isn’t for live calls, and for legal, medical or emergency communication the original signer or a qualified interpreter should be used.

How long does it take?

About a minute of GPU time per second of video. An 11.5-second video took about 12 minutes.

What photo works best?

A neutral, front-facing photo. The photo’s pose and smile can carry into the first frames.

How much does it cost to use Morph?

In Canvas, 5 credits per second of video at 720p or 3 at 480p, with a 10-second minimum, charged only when a render succeeds. Through the API, $0.12 per second at 720p or $0.06 at 480p.
